We present JEV-Star, a StarCraft II controller that combines frequent JEV action selection with persistent GPT-6 Astra planning. We distinguish five macro configurations: pure random, JEV-only, Astra-constrained random, Astra-constrained JEV, and Astra-advisory JEV. Four configurations have ten-game realtime evaluations against the strongest non-cheating built-in opponent, Lv7. Constrained JEV wins 9/10 games; advisory JEV wins 3/10, loses three, and reaches the 20-minute limit four times. Pure random and constrained random record no wins; the latter includes one human-adjudicated loss without a saved replay. A matched Lv7 JEV-only batch is not available. The game continues during inference: all 15,505 returned JEV decisions and 344 returned Astra plans in the two JEV-planner batches span advancing game frames. Median JEV latency is 0.375 seconds with constraints and 0.421 seconds with advisory plans. Across 35 micromanagement maps, the historical planned controller raises mean enemy elimination from 16.50% to 37.69% and wins from 3 to 7 out of 105 episodes. These development samples demonstrate successful realtime hierarchical control while leaving version differences, small samples, and the missing matched JEV-only baseline as limitations. Code and derived evidence are available at https://github.com/sc2musa/Jev_Star.
